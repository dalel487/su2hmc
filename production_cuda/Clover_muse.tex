\chapter[Clover Fermions]{Symanzik Improved Clover Fermions}\label{Chap:Clover}
	In section \ref{Subsub:Wilson} we discussed how Wilson Fermions have $\mathscr{O}(a)$ discretisation errors and in
	\ref{Subsub:Wilson gauge action} the Wilson gauge action has $\mathscr{O}\left(a^2\right)$. Additionally, at higher
	chemical potentials these errors can become significant enough to appear as lattice artefacts, putting an upper limit
	on the how high we can push the chemical potential. The standard method to remove these is to conduct multiple
	simulations at different lattice spacings, then extrapolate to zero lattice spacing. However in order to maintain the
	same lattice volume one must also change the number of lattice sites in the spatial extent, further increasing the
	computational cost.

	In section \ref{Sub:Artefacts} we looked at $\xi=\frac{1}{a m_\text{PS}}$. At higher densities though a
	different scale
	\begin{equation}\label{Eq:Dense_Scale}
		\xi_\mu=\frac{1}{a\mu}
	\end{equation}
	becomes sufficiently large to introduce \acrshort{ir} artefacts. This restricts how dense we can simulate at,
	preventing us from comparing results with other approaches such as perturbation theory. If however we could remove
	these lattice artefacts we can then simulate at higher densities, and potentially compare with two-colour
	perturbation theory. This chapter will discuss how we go about this.
\section{Symanzik Improvement}\label{Sec:Symanzik}
	Another approach which I will now motivate is to use an action with higher order discretisation errors, with a common
	approach being \emph{Symanzik Improvement}\index{Symanzik Improvement}\cite{Symanzik:1983dc,Symanzik:1983gh}. Let us
	look again at equation \eqref{Eq:Correlation_Ineq} from section \ref{Sub:Artefacts} $$a\ll\xi\ll L\text{.}$$
	As is described in \cite{Symanzik:1983dc}, physical observables depending on the lattice spacing $a$ and gauge
	coupling $g$ can be written in the form
	\begin{equation}\label{Eq:Phys_Symanzik}
		P(g,a)=\xi^{-D}
		K_P\left[1+\mathscr{O}\left(\left(\frac{a}{\xi}\right)^2\ln\left(\frac{\xi}{a}\right)\right)\right]
	\end{equation}
	where $D$ is the number of dimensions and $K_P$ is a universal constant. The correction term depends on both the
	choice of lattice action, $\xi$, and $a$. If we were to attempt to reduce this error by increasing $\xi$, we will end
	up with \acrshort{ir} artefacts as $\xi\rightarrow L$.

	However, if we could improve the action so that we instead have corrections of the form
	\begin{equation}\label{Eq:Symanzik_Correction}
		\mathscr{O}\left(\left(\frac{a}{\xi}\right)^4\ln\left(\frac{\xi}{a}\right)\right)
	\end{equation}
	we would be able to simulate at smaller spacings or higher densities without encountering lattice artefacts.

\section{Clover}	
	Another approach is to use an action with higher order discretisation errors, with a common approach being
	\emph{Symanzik Improvement}\index{Symanzik Improvement}.
\subsection{Clover Fermions}
	The Clover improved Wilson action can be given as
	\begin{equation}
		S_\text{I}=S_\text{Wilson}+S_\text{Clov}=S_\text{Wilson}
		-\frac{c_\text{SW}}{2} \kappa a^5 \sum\limits_{n\in\Lambda}\sum\limits_{\mu<\nu}
		\bar{\psi}(n)\frac{1}{2}\sigma_{\mu\nu}F_{\mu\nu}(n)\psi(n)
	\end{equation}
	where $c_\text{SW}$ is the clover or Sheikholeslami-Wohlert coefficient, $\sigma_{\mu\nu}$ is the commutator of the
	Euclidean Dirac matrices in the chiral representation and the clover term at site $n$ in the $\mu-\nu$ direction is
	given by
	\begin{equation}\label{Eq:Clover}
		F_{\mu\nu}(n)=\frac{-i}{8a^2}\left(Q_{\mu\nu}(n)-Q_{\nu\mu}(n)\right)
	\end{equation}
	where (at least in our implementation, it is not unique)
	\begin{figure}
		\centring
		\begin{adjustbox}{width=\linewidth}
			\tikzfig{Tikz/Clover}
		\end{adjustbox}
		\captionsetup{width=\linewidth}
		\caption{A half-clover $Q_{\mu\nu}(n)$. The full clover term consists of this minus its hermitian conjugate}
		\label{Tikz:Clover}
	\end{figure}
	\begin{equation}\label{Eq:Half Clover}
		Q_{\mu\nu}(n)=U_{\mu\nu}(n)+U_{\nu,-\mu}(n)+U_{-\nu,\mu}(n)+U_{-\mu,-\nu}(n)
	\end{equation}
	and $U_{\mu\nu}$ is the plaquette given in \cref{Eq:Plaquette} (see \cref{Tikz:Clover}). Since the
	$Q_{\mu\nu}$ terms are the sum of four plaquettes they should have the same form as $U_\mu$ given in \cref{Eq:SU(2)
	Def} (but are \emph{not} an element of $SU(2)$), from here it follows that
	\begin{equation}\label{Eq:Diff_form}
		Q_{\mu\nu}(n)-Q_{\nu\mu}(n)=\begin{pmatrix}
		\alpha-\bar{\alpha} & \beta+\beta \\
		-\bar{\beta}-\bar{\beta} & \bar{\alpha}-\alpha \end{pmatrix}=
		\begin{pmatrix} 2di & 2c+2bi \\ -2c+2bi & -2di \end{pmatrix}
	\end{equation}
	which is anti-Hermitian. Multiplying out front by $-i$ gives us 
	\begin{equation}\label{Eq:Clov_Form}
		F_{\mu\nu}(n)=\frac{1}{8a^2}
		\begin{pmatrix} 2d & 2b-2ci \\ 2b+2ci & -2d \end{pmatrix}\text{.}
	\end{equation}
	which is hermitian and traceless (i.e. of the same form as an element of the Lie Algebra).
\section{Clover Force Update}
	For the clover force update with respect to a link $U_\mu$ is similar to the procedure described in 
	\cref{Subsub:Fermion Force}. Only the leaves containing $U_\mu(n)$ or $U^\dagger_{\mu}(n)$
	contribute. It does have the additional complication that there are six clovers containing the gauge link between $n$
	and $n+\hat{\mu}$ or the conjugate link between $n+\hat{\mu}$ and $n$.
	\begin{enumerate}
		\item The clover at the site $n$ itself contains the link in the $+\mu,+\nu$ leaf and the $+\mu,-\nu$ leaf.
		\item The clover at the site $n+\hat{\mu}$ contains the link in the $-\mu,+\nu$ leaf and the $-\mu,-\nu$ leaf.
		\item The clover at the site $n+\hat{\nu}$ contains the link in the $+\mu,-\nu$ leaf.
		\item The clover at the site $n-\hat{\nu}$ contains the link in the $+\mu,+\nu$ leaf.
		\item The clover at the site $n+\hat{\mu}+\hat{\nu}$ contains the link in the $-\mu,-\nu$ leaf.
		\item The clover at the site $n+\hat{\mu}-\hat{\nu}$ contains the link in the $-\mu,+\nu$ leaf.
	\end{enumerate}
	\begin{figure}
		\centring
		\begin{adjustbox}{width=\linewidth}
			\tikzfig{Tikz/Clover_Force_All}
		\end{adjustbox}
		\captionsetup{width=\linewidth}
		\caption{Leaves containing the link $U_\mu(n)$ or $U^\dagger_{\mu}(n)$. Note that all the
		clovers drawn in the diagram are $Q_{\mu\nu}(n)$. The contribution from $Q_{\nu\mu}(n)$ is not shown.}
		\label{Tikz:Clover_Force}
	\end{figure}

	Unlike the Wilson case where the generator multiplies the plaquette from the left, the location of the generator in
	the clover force depends on the location of the link in each clover. For the $Q_{\mu\nu}(n)$ leaves seen in 
	\cref{Tikz:Clover_Force}.
	\begin{enumerate}
		\item The clover at the site $n$ itself has the generator multiplying its top-right leaf from the left (w.r.t. $U_\mu(n)$)
				and bottom right leaf from the right (w.r.t. $U^\dagger_\mu(n)$).
		\item The clover at the site $n+\hat{\mu}$ has the generator appearing between the third and fourth link of its
				top left leaf ($\nu,-\mu$) (w.r.t. $U_\mu(n)$) and between the first and second link of its bottom left leaf
				($-\mu,-\nu$) (w.r.t. $U^\dagger_\mu(n)$).
		\item The clover at the site $n+\hat{\nu}$ has the generator appearing between the first and second link (w.r.t. $U_\mu(n)$)
		\item The clover at the site $n-\hat{\nu}$ has the generator appearing between the third and fourth link
				(w.r.t. $U^\dagger_\mu(n)$).
		\item The clover at the site $n+\hat{\mu}+\hat{\nu}$ has the generator appearing between second and third link (w.r.t. $U_\mu(n)$).
		\item The clover at the site $n+\hat{\mu}-\hat{\nu}$ has the generator appearing between second and third link
				(w.r.t. $U^\dagger_\mu(n)$).
	\end{enumerate}

	Looking at the $F_{\mu\nu}(n)$ and the derivatives with respect to $U_\mu(n)$, we see that the derivative from each
	generator takes the form of an $SU(2)$ group element
	\begin{align}
		\frac{\partial U_{\mu\nu}(n)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}&=
		i\sigma_x U_\mu(n)U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)U^\dagger_\mu\left(n+\hat{\nu}\right)U^\dagger_\nu(n)\nonumber\\
		&=\begin{pmatrix} -i\bar{\beta} & i\bar{\alpha} \\i\alpha & i\beta\end{pmatrix}=
		\begin{pmatrix} -b-ci & d+ai \\ -d+ai & -b+ci\end{pmatrix}\label{Eq:Sig_x_deriv}\\
		\frac{\partial U_{\mu\nu}(n)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}&=
		i\sigma_y U_\mu(n)U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)U^\dagger_\mu\left(n+\hat{\nu}\right)U^\dagger_\nu(n)\nonumber\\
		&=\begin{pmatrix} \bar{\beta} & -\bar{\alpha} \\\alpha & \beta\end{pmatrix}=
		\begin{pmatrix} c-bi & -a+di \\ a+di & c+bi\end{pmatrix}\label{Eq:Sig_y_deriv}\\
		\frac{\partial U_{\mu\nu}(n)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}&=
		i\sigma_z U_\mu(n)U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)U^\dagger_\mu\left(n+\hat{\nu}\right)U^\dagger_\nu(n)\nonumber\\
		&=\begin{pmatrix} i\alpha & i\beta \\i\bar{\beta} & -i\bar{\alpha}\end{pmatrix}=
		\begin{pmatrix} -d+ai & -b+ci \\ b+ci & -d-ai\end{pmatrix}\label{Eq:Sig_z_deriv}\text{.}
	\end{align}
	\begin{equation}\label{Eq:Site_Leaf_Conj Deriv}
		\frac{\partial U^\dagger_{\mu\nu}(n)}{\partial U^\dagger_\mu(n)}=
		-i U_\nu(n)U_\mu\left(n+\hat{\nu}\right)U^\dagger_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)U^\dagger_\mu(n)\lambda_j=
		\left(\frac{\partial U_{\mu\nu}(n)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}\right)^\dagger\text{.}
	\end{equation}
	We can apply this symmetry to the $U_{-\nu,\mu}(n)$ leaf to obtain $\frac{\partial U_{\mu,-\nu}(n)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}$
	\begin{equation}\label{Eq:Deriv_Symm}
		\frac{\partial U_{-\nu,\mu}(n)}{\partial U^\dagger_\mu(n)}=
		\left(\frac{\partial U^\dagger_{-\nu,\mu}(n)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}\right)^\dagger\Leftrightarrow
		\frac{\partial U_{\mu,-\nu}(n)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}=
		\frac{\partial U^\dagger_{-\nu,\mu}(n)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}=
		\left(\frac{\partial U_{-\nu,\mu}(n)}{\partial U^\dagger_\mu(n)}\right)^\dagger\text{.}
	\end{equation}

	Using this symmetry and getting the difference yields
	\begin{multline}\label{Eq:Clov_Force_1}
		\frac{\partial F_{\mu\nu}(n)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}=\frac{-i}{8a}\left(\frac{\partial Q_{\mu\nu}(n)}
		{\partial U_\mu(n)}-\frac{\partial Q_{\nu\mu}(n)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}\right)\\
		=\frac{-i}{8a}\left(\frac{\partial U_{\mu\nu}(n)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}
		-\frac{\partial U_{\mu,-\nu}(n)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}\right)
		=\frac{-i}{8a}\left(\frac{\partial U_{\mu\nu}(n)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}
		-\left(\frac{\partial U_{-\nu,\mu}(n)}{\partial U^\dagger_\mu(n)}\right)^\dagger\right)
	\end{multline}
	as the total contribution to the derivative of the clover centred at $n$ with respect to $U_\mu(n)$. Repeating for
	the other contributions (see \cref{Tikz:Clover_Force_Comp})
	\begin{align}
		%n+mu
		\frac{\partial F_{\mu\nu}(n+\hat{\mu})}{\partial U_\mu(n)}&=
		\frac{-i}{8a}\left(\frac{\partial Q_{\mu\nu}\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}-\frac{\partial
		Q_{\nu\mu}\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}\right)\nonumber\\
		&=\frac{-i}{8a}\left(\frac{\partial U_{\nu,-\mu}\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}
		-\frac{\partial U_{-\nu,-\mu}\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}\right)\nonumber\\
		&=\frac{-i}{8a}\left(\frac{\partial U_{\nu,-\mu}\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}
		-\left(\frac{\partial U_{-\mu,-\nu}\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)}{\partial U^\dagger_\mu(n)}\right)^\dagger\right)\label{Eq:Clov_Force_2}\\
		% n+nu
		\frac{\partial F_{\mu\nu}\left(n+\hat{\nu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}&=
			\frac{-i}{8a}\frac{\partial Q_{\mu\nu}\left(n+\hat{\nu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}=
			\frac{-i}{8a}\frac{\partial U_{-\nu,\mu}\left(n+\hat{\nu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}\label{Eq:Clov_Force_3}\\
		% n-nu
		\frac{\partial F_{\mu\nu}\left(n-\hat{\nu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}&=
			\frac{i}{8a}\frac{\partial Q_{\nu\mu}\left(n-\hat{\nu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}\nonumber\\
			&=\frac{i}{8a}\frac{\partial U_{\nu\mu}\left(n-\hat{\nu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}=
			\frac{i}{8a}\left(\frac{\partial U_{\mu\nu}\left(n-\hat{\nu}\right)}
			{\partial U^\dagger_\mu(n)}\right)^\dagger\label{Eq:Clov_Force_4}\\
		% n+mu+nu
		\frac{\partial F_{\mu\nu}\left(n+\hat{\mu}+\hat{\nu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}&=
			\frac{-i}{8a}\frac{\partial Q_{\mu\nu}\left(n+\hat{\mu}+\hat{\nu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}=
			\frac{-i}{8a}\frac{\partial U_{-\mu,-\nu}\left(n+\hat{\mu}+\hat{\nu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}\label{Eq:Clov_Force_5}\\
		% n+mu-nu
		\frac{\partial F_{\mu\nu}\left(n+\hat{\mu}-\hat{\nu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}&=
			\frac{i}{8a}\frac{\partial Q_{\nu\mu}\left(n+\hat{\mu}-\hat{\nu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}\nonumber\\
			&=\frac{i}{8a}\frac{\partial U_{-\mu,\nu}\left(n+\hat{\mu}-\hat{\nu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}
			=\frac{i}{8a}\left(\frac{\partial U_{\nu,-\mu}\left(n+\hat{\mu}-\hat{\nu}\right)}
			{\partial U^\dagger_\mu(n)}\right)^\dagger\label{Eq:Clov_Force_6}\text{.}
	\end{align}

	Next, we need to consider the contribution from the derivative with respect to
	$U^\dagger_\mu(n)$. The procedure for this is similar to that for 
	\cref{Eq:Clov_Force_1,Eq:Clov_Force_2,Eq:Clov_Force_3,Eq:Clov_Force_4,Eq:Clov_Force_5,Eq:Clov_Force_6}.
	Focusing only on $F_{\mu\nu}(n)$ and using \cref{Eq:Deriv_Symm}
	\begin{multline}\label{Eq:Clover_deriv_dag}
		\frac{\partial F_{\mu\nu}(n)}{\partial U^\dagger_\mu(n)}=
		\frac{-i}{8a}\left(\frac{\partial Q_{\mu\nu}(n)} {\partial U^\dagger_\mu(n)}
		-\frac{\partial Q_{\nu\mu}(n)}{\partial U^\dagger_\mu(n)}\right)\\
		=\frac{-i}{8a}\left(\frac{\partial U_{\nu,-\mu}(n)}{\partial U^\dagger_\mu(n)}-
			\frac{\partial U_{\nu\mu}(n)}{\partial U^\dagger_\mu(n)}\right)
		=\frac{-i}{8a}\left(\frac{\partial U_{\nu,-\mu}(n)}{\partial U^\dagger_\mu(n)}-
			\left(\frac{\partial U_{\mu\nu}(n)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}\right)^\dagger\right)\text{.}
	\end{multline}
	Comparing to \cref{Eq:Clov_Force_1}, we can quickly see that
	\begin{equation}\label{Eq:NoDag_vs_Dag}
		\frac{\partial F_{\mu\nu}(n)}{\partial U^\dagger_\mu(n)}=
		\left(\frac{\partial F_{\mu\nu}(n)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}\right)^\dagger
	\end{equation}
	and thus from \cref{Eq:Sig_x_deriv} the overall form of the derivatives in the $\hat{\mu}$ direction is
	\begin{equation}\label{Eq:Final_Clov_Form}
		\frac{\partial F_{\mu\nu}(n)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}+
		\frac{\partial F_{\mu\nu}(n)}{\partial U^\dagger_\mu(n)}=
		\frac{-i}{8a}\begin{pmatrix} -i\bar{\beta} +i\beta& i\bar{\alpha}-i\bar{\alpha} \\
		i\alpha-i\alpha & i\beta-i\bar{\beta}\end{pmatrix}=
		\frac{1}{8a}\begin{pmatrix} 2bi & 0 \\ 0 & 2bi\end{pmatrix}
	\end{equation}
	which is anti-hermitian. Comparing this with \cref{Eq:Pauli SU(2)}, we see that the force from the $\sigma_x$
	generator only consists of the $\sigma_x$ components of the leaves involved in the force. Using a similar approach we
	can also show that the $\sigma_y$ and $\sigma_z$ components behave in the same manner.
	\subsection{Worked example}
	Let us now consider a case where all the links are set to $\ID$ except $U_\mu(x)$ and $U_\nu\left(x+\hat{\mu}\right)$.
	Then we observe that
	\begin{align}
		\frac{\partial F_{\mu\nu}(n)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}&= \frac{-i}{8a}\left(\frac{\partial U_{\mu\nu}(n)}{\partial
		U_\mu(n)}-\left(\frac{\partial U_{-\nu,\mu}(n)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}\right)^\dagger\right)\nonumber\\ & =
		\frac{-i}{8a}\left(i\lambda_jU_\mu(n)U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)\ID\ID-\left(\ID\ID\ID
		U^\dagger_\mu(n)\left(-i\lambda_j\right)^\dagger\right)^\dagger\right)\nonumber\\&= \frac{-i}{8a}\left(i\lambda_j
		U_\mu(n)U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)-\left(i\lambda_j U_\mu(n)\right)\right)\nonumber\\&=
		\frac{1}{8a}\lambda_j U_\mu(n)\left(U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)-\ID\right)\label{Eq:WE1}\\
		\frac{\partial F_{\mu\nu}\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}&=
		\frac{-i}{8a}\left(\frac{\partial U_{\nu,-\mu}\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}-\left(\frac{\partial
		U_{-\nu,-\mu}\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}\right)^\dagger\right)\nonumber\\&=
		\frac{-i}{8a}\left(U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)\ID\ID\left(i\lambda_j
		U_\mu(n)\right)-\left(U^\dagger_\mu(n)\left(-i\lambda_j\right)\ID\ID\ID\right)^\dagger\right)\nonumber\\&=
		\frac{-i}{8a}\left(iU_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)\lambda_jU_\mu(n)-\left(-iU^\dagger_\mu(n)\lambda_j\right)^\dagger\right)\nonumber\\&=
		\frac{-i}{8a}\left(iU_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)\lambda_jU_\mu(n)-i\lambda_jU_\mu(n)\right)\nonumber\\&=
		\frac{1}{8a}\left(U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)-\ID\right)\lambda_j U_\mu(n)\label{Eq:WE2}\\
		\frac{\partial F_{\mu\nu}\left(n+\hat{\nu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}&=
		\frac{-i}{8a}\frac{\partial U_{-\nu,\mu}\left(n+\hat{\nu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}\nonumber\\&=
		\frac{-i}{8a}\ID\left(i\lambda_j\right)U_\mu(n)U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)\ID\nonumber\\&=
		\frac{1}{8a}\lambda_j U_\mu(n)U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)\label{Eq:WE3}\\
		\frac{\partial F_{\mu\nu}\left(n-\hat{\nu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}&=
		\frac{+i}{8a}\frac{\partial U_{\nu\mu}\left(n-\hat{\nu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}\nonumber\\&=
		\frac{+i}{8a}\left(\ID\ID\ID U^\dagger_\mu(n)\left(-\lambda_j\right)\right)\nonumber\\&=
		\frac{i}{8a}\left(-i U^\dagger_\mu(n)\lambda_j\right)^\dagger\nonumber\\&=
		\frac{-1}{8a}\lambda_j U_\mu(n)\label{Eq:WE4}\\
		\frac{\partial F_{\mu\nu}\left(n+\hat{\mu}+\hat{\nu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}&=
		\frac{-i}{8a}\frac{\partial U_{-\mu,-\nu}\left(n+\hat{\mu}+\hat{\nu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}\nonumber\\&=
		\frac{-i}{8a}\ID\ID\left(i\lambda_j\right)U_\mu(n)U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)\nonumber\\&=
		\frac{1}{8a}\lambda_j U_\mu(n)U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)\label{Eq:WE5}\\
		\frac{\partial F_{\mu\nu}\left(n+\hat{\mu}-\hat{\nu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}&=
		\frac{+i}{8a}\frac{\partial U_{-\mu,\nu}\left(n+\hat{\mu}-\hat{\nu}\right)}{\partial U_\mu(n)}\nonumber\\&=
		\frac{i}{8a}\left(\ID\ID U^\dagger_\mu(n)\left(-i\lambda_j\right)\ID\right)^\dagger\nonumber\\&=
		\frac{i}{8a}\left(-i U^\dagger_\mu(n)\lambda_j\right)^\dagger\nonumber\\&=
		\frac{-1}{8a}\lambda_j U_\mu(n)\label{Eq:WE6}
	\end{align}
	Summing \cref{Eq:WE1,Eq:WE2,Eq:WE3,Eq:WE4,Eq:WE5,Eq:WE6} yields
	\begin{multline}\label{Eq:WE_Force_Cont_Sum}
		\frac{1}{8a}\lambda_j U_\mu(n)\left(U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)-\ID\right)+
		\frac{1}{8a}\left(U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)-\ID\right)\lambda_j U_\mu(n)+
		\frac{1}{8a}\lambda_j U_\mu(n)U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)\\-
		\frac{1}{8a}\lambda_j U_\mu(n)+
		\frac{1}{8a}\lambda_j U_\mu(n)U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)-
		\frac{1}{8a}\lambda_j U_\mu(n)\\=
		\frac{1}{8a}\lambda_jU_\mu(n)U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)-\frac{1}{8a}\lambda_jU_\mu(n)+
		\frac{1}{8a}U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)\lambda_jU_\mu(n)-\frac{1}{8a}\lambda_jU_\mu(n)\\+
		\frac{1}{4a}\lambda_j U_\mu(n)U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)-\frac{1}{4a}\lambda_j U_\mu(n)\\=
		\frac{3}{8a}\lambda_j U_\mu(n)U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)+
		\frac{1}{8a}U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)\lambda_j U_\mu(n)-
		\frac{1}{2a}\lambda_j U_\mu(n)\text{.}
	\end{multline}
	Thus the total force is obtained by subtracting off the hermitan conjugate of \cref{Eq:WE_Force_Cont_Sum}.
	\begin{multline}\label{Eq:WE_Total_Force}
		\frac{3}{8a}\left(\lambda_j U_\mu(n)U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)-
		U^\dagger_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)U^\dagger_\mu(n)\lambda_j\right)\\
		+\frac{1}{8a}\left( U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)\lambda_j U_\mu(n)-
		U^\dagger_\mu(n)\lambda_j U^\dagger_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right) \right)\\
		- \frac{1}{2a}\left(\lambda_j U_\mu(n)-U^\dagger_\mu(n)\lambda_j\right)
	\end{multline}
	As a sanity check, one can set $U_\mu(n)=U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)=\ID$. In that case the total force is $0$ as
	expected. Likewise setting only $U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)=\ID$ yields
	\begin{equation}\label{Eq:WE_Total_Force_1}
		\frac{3}{8a}\left(\lambda_j U_\mu(n)-U^\dagger_\mu(n)\lambda_j\right)
		+\frac{1}{8a}\left(\lambda_j U_\mu(n)- U^\dagger_\mu(n)\lambda_j\right)
		- \frac{1}{2a}\left(\lambda_j U_\mu(n)-U^\dagger_\mu(n)\lambda_j\right)=0\text{.}
	\end{equation}
	If however we set $U_\mu(n)=\ID$ instead we obtain
	\begin{multline}\label{Eq:WE_Total_Force_2}
		\frac{3}{8a}\left(\lambda_jU_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)-
		U^\dagger_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)\lambda_j\right)
		+\frac{1}{8a}\left( U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)\lambda_j-
		\lambda_j U^\dagger_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right) \right)
		- \frac{1}{2a}\left(\lambda_j -\lambda_j\right)\\=
		\frac{3}{8a}\left(\lambda_jU_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)-
		U^\dagger_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)\lambda_j\right)
		+\frac{1}{8a}\left( U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)\lambda_j-
		\lambda_j U^\dagger_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right) \right)
	\end{multline}
	which is not trivially $0$. Considering $U_\nu\left(n+\hat{\mu}\right)=i\sigma_x$ as an example we can consider what
	the contribution should be for $\lambda_j=\sigma_x$, $\lambda_j=\sigma_y$ and $\lambda_j=\sigma_z$. 
	\begin{align}
		\frac{3}{8a}\left(\sigma_x\left(i\sigma_x\right)-\left(-i\sigma_x\right)\sigma_x\right)
		+\frac{1}{8a}\left( \left(i\sigma_x\right)\sigma_x-\sigma_x\left(-i\sigma_x\right)\right)&=
		\frac{3}{8a}\left(2i\ID\right)+\frac{1}{8a}\left(2i\ID\right)\nonumber\\&=\frac{i}{a}\ID\\
		\frac{3}{8a}\left(\sigma_y\left(i\sigma_x\right)-\left(-i\sigma_x\right)\sigma_y\right)
		+\frac{1}{8a}\left(\left(i\sigma_x\right)\sigma_y-\sigma_y\left(-i\sigma_x\right)\right)&=
		\frac{3}{8a}\left(\sigma_z-\sigma_z\right)+\frac{1}{8a}\left(\sigma_z-\sigma_z\right)\nonumber\\&=0\\
		\frac{3}{8a}\left(\sigma_z\left(i\sigma_x\right)-\left(-i\sigma_x\right)\sigma_z\right)
		+\frac{1}{8a}\left(\left(i\sigma_x\right)\sigma_z-\sigma_z\left(-i\sigma_x\right)\right)&=
		\frac{3}{8a}\left(-\sigma_y+\sigma_y\right)+\frac{1}{8a}\left(-\sigma_y+\sigma_y\right)\nonumber\\&=0
	\end{align}
	which is consistent with \cref{Eq:Final_Clov_Form}, keeping in mind that \cref{Eq:Final_Clov_Form} was only looking
	at the contribution from a single clover containing $U_\mu(n)$ and not all clovers.
